## SECTION 1: EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**Organization:** Brilliant Unicorn LLC  
**Country:** Kenya  
**Focus Area:** E-waste management from clean energy sector  
**Requested Amount:** EUR 200,000–350,000 (grant + repayable grant)  
**Co-financing:** 30% ($85,000–$150,000 in-kind + cash)  
**Duration:** 24 months  

**Problem:** Kenya's clean energy boom (solar home systems, mini-grids, e-mobility) is generating a tidal wave of e-waste. By 2030, an estimated 50,000 tons of solar battery waste will need disposal. Currently:
- No tracking system exists for end-of-life hardware
- 80% of e-waste is informally recycled (burning, acid leaching)
- Clean energy companies face reputational and regulatory risk
- EEP Africa's 2026 Kenya mandate explicitly requires e-waste solutions

**Solution:** Kudu Stack extends its Fortress immutable ledger to track clean energy hardware from deployment through disposal. Combined with AlphaCore's predictive maintenance engine, we:
1. **Predict failure** before it happens (reduce premature disposal)
2. **Track lifecycle** immutably (prove compliance to regulators and funders)
3. **Route disposal** to certified recyclers (prevent informal dumping)
4. **Verify circularity** for carbon credit eligibility (turn waste into value)

**Innovation:** We are not a recycling company. We are the **software infrastructure** that makes circular clean energy possible. Our immutable ledger + edge AI turns e-waste compliance from a cost center into a competitive advantage.

## SECTION 2: PROJECT DESCRIPTION

### 2.1 Background

Kenya's clean energy sector has grown explosively:
- 500,000+ solar home systems deployed (2020–2025)
- 50+ mini-grid operators active
- 1,000+ e-mobility vehicles on roads
- But: No national e-waste policy for clean energy; no tracking infrastructure

### 2.2 Objectives

1. **Deploy e-waste tracking module** for 500 clean energy devices (solar batteries, inverters, e-mobility batteries)
2. **Reduce premature disposal by 25%** via AlphaCore predictive maintenance
3. **Route 80% of end-of-life devices** to certified recyclers (vs. 20% currently)
4. **Issue 1,000 disposal certificates** on Fortress ledger for compliance

### 2.3 Activities

**Phase 1: Foundation (Months 1–6)**
- Extend Fortress ledger with e-waste asset schema
- Build AlphaCore predictive maintenance module for battery health
- Partner with 3 clean energy companies (solar SHS, mini-grid, e-mobility)
- Integrate with 2 certified e-waste recyclers (WEEE Centre, E-waste Solutions Kenya)

**Phase 2: Pilot (Months 7–14)**
- Deploy tracking on 500 devices across partner networks
- Train 10 technicians on predictive maintenance alerts
- Generate first disposal certificates
- Conduct baseline vs. intervention comparison

**Phase 3: Scale (Months 15–24)**
- Open API for other clean energy companies
- Integrate with Kenya Bureau of Standards (KEBS) e-waste registry
- Publish open standard for clean energy e-waste tracking
- Apply for carbon credit methodology (e-waste avoidance = avoided emissions)

### 2.4 Target Beneficiaries

| Group | Benefit |
|---|---|
| Clean energy companies | Compliance, reduced disposal costs, brand protection |
| Recyclers | Verified feedstock, higher margins |
| Communities | Reduced toxic exposure from informal recycling |
| Regulators (NEMA, KEBS) | Real-time e-waste visibility |
| Carbon markets | Verified circularity data for credit issuance |
