## THE CHALLENGE

East African conservancies manage 60%+ of Kenya's wildlife outside national parks. Yet they rely on:
- Annual ground censuses (expensive, slow, inaccurate)
- Paper-based carbon credit reporting (vulnerable to fraud)
- No real-time poaching detection (reactive, not preventive)

The result: funders distrust conservation outcomes. Carbon buyers pay for forests that may already be gone.

## THE INNOVATION

**Kudu Biotech** deploys low-cost, solar-powered sensor nodes that:
1. **Monitor biomass** via multispectral imaging + IoT sensors
2. **Detect species** via edge AI (no cloud dependency)
3. **Record proof** on an immutable Rust ledger (no double-counting)

## WHY IT WORKS FOR EAST AFRICA

- **Low connectivity:** Edge AI processes data locally. Only summaries sync via satellite.
- **Unreliable power:** Solar + battery + AlphaCore's power-flicker resilience.
- **Local capacity:** Built by a Kenyan engineer. No foreign vendor lock-in.
- **Cost efficiency:** $500/node vs. $5,000+ for commercial alternatives.
