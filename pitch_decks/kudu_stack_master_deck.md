## SLIDE 2: THE PROBLEM — Three Trust Gaps

### 1. The Carbon Trust Gap
- Carbon buyers pay for forest protection, but receive PDF reports.
- Deforestation happens silently. By the time it's detected, credits are worthless.
- EU Green Transition Directive (2026) now criminalizes false environmental claims.

### 2. The Biodiversity Trust Gap  
- Endangered species funding is released on annual census data.
- Poaching events are discovered days or weeks later.
- No real-time "Proof of Life" for sponsors.

### 3. The Water-Energy Trust Gap
- Drought-prone counties lose 30-40% of water to leaks and theft.
- Desalination and smart irrigation require energy — but no one models the trade-off locally.
- UN declared 2026 the "era of global water bankruptcy."
