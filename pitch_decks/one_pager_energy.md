## THE CHALLENGE

Kenya's water utilities lose 30–40% of supply to leaks, theft, and unmetered use. Meanwhile:
- Desalination and smart irrigation are energy-intensive — but no one models the trade-off locally.
- Mini-grid operators lack predictive tools for demand + supply matching.
- E-waste from solar batteries is becoming a crisis (EEP Africa 2026 Kenya mandate).

## THE INNOVATION

**AquaSense + AlphaCore** provide:
1. **Local water risk scoring** — AI rainfall forecasting + leak detection simulation
2. **Adaptation planning** — Low-energy desalination feasibility + smart irrigation ROI
3. **Infrastructure resilience** — AlphaCore runs on unreliable power and connectivity

## THE E-WASTE ANGLE (EEP Africa 2026)

Kudu's sensor nodes are designed for **circular lifecycle management**:
- Modular hardware (swap, don't trash)
- AlphaCore tracks device health and predicts failure
- End-of-life battery routing to certified recyclers
- Immutable ledger proves responsible disposal for carbon credit eligibility

## WHY IT WORKS FOR KENYA

- **County-level:** Water boards can license AquaSense per-county.
- **Mini-grid:** Operators use AlphaCore for demand forecasting on weak grids.
- **E-waste compliance:** EEP Africa's 2026 Kenya focus is exactly this problem.
