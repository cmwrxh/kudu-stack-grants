## M&E PLAN

### Data Collection
- **Automated:** All telemetry, ledger writes, and AI inferences are self-logging
- **Quarterly surveys:** Ranger satisfaction, community perception
- **Annual audits:** Third-party carbon credit verification, water board assessment

### Reporting
- **Monthly:** Technical performance dashboard (automated)
- **Quarterly:** Funder progress report (narrative + metrics)
- **Annually:** Impact evaluation with independent verification

### Learning & Adaptation
- **Chaos engineering:** Weekly failure simulations identify weaknesses before they matter
- **Model retraining:** Sentinel AI models updated quarterly with new field data
- **Feature requests:** Ranger app includes direct feedback channel to engineering
